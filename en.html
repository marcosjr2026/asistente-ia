The service

What the assistant does

  • Answers the inbound calls you choose to forward to it: for example, when nobody picks up, when the line is busy or after hours.
  • Introduces itself as your business's AI virtual assistant and says in its first sentence that the call is recorded and transcribed.
  • If the caller doesn't want to be recorded, it offers another way to reach your team.
  • Answers only with the information you approved, such as your hours or address.
  • Takes the caller's name, phone number and reason for calling, then takes the appointment or visit request for your team to confirm, or leaves a message for someone on your team.
  • Emails you a summary of each call.
  • Can answer in English and Spanish. During the pilot you hear how it sounds and decide.
  • If the caller would rather talk to a person, it takes a message so your team can call back. In an emergency, it tells the caller to dial 911.
The pilot

How the free 14-day pilot works

  1. Free assessment. Fill out the short form and we review together how calls reach your business today.
  2. One-page agreement. Before anything is turned on, we sign a simple agreement that explains what the assistant does and does not do.
  3. You approve the script. You review and approve what the assistant will say, in English and Spanish, including the opening notice.
  4. You decide which calls to forward. You pick the lines or hours. You can keep your voicemail as a backup.
  5. 14-day trial. You get a summary of each call by email. At the end, you decide whether to continue. No cost and no commitment.

The pilot covers inbound voice calls only. The assistant does not make outbound calls or send text messages.

Use cases

Examples for Miami-Dade businesses

Accountants and tax preparers

During tax season many calls come in at once, or while you are meeting with a client.

“I'd like an appointment for my tax return.” The assistant takes the name, phone number and a one-line topic, and takes the appointment request for your team to confirm, or leaves a message.

It does not ask for Social Security numbers, ITINs, bank details or amounts, and it does not give tax advice.

Travel agencies

Questions about a destination, a ticket or your hours, while your team is helping someone in the office.

“I want to fly to Bogotá in December.” The assistant notes the request and contact details so an agent can call back.

It does not ask for cards or passports, does not quote prices and does not confirm bookings.

Shipping agencies

Calls asking what can be shipped or your hours, while your team is receiving boxes at the counter.

“I want to send a box to Venezuela.” The assistant takes the details and the visit request for your team to confirm, or leaves a message for your team.

It does not ask for cards or ID documents, and it does not give prices, delivery dates or tracking information.

Privacy

Sensitive data: the assistant does not ask for it

The assistant is set up to never ask for credit or debit card numbers, passports or ID documents, Social Security numbers, ITINs or other tax data, bank accounts or health information. If a caller starts to share them, the assistant does not ask further or repeat them, and the message only notes the general topic. It also does not give legal, tax or any other advice: your team handles that.

For now, the service is not available to medical offices or other healthcare providers.
